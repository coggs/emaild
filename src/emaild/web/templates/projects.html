Projects

An umbrella is something you're involved in that never finishes (a committee, the household); its sub-projects are goals with an end. emAIl files the matching emails, notes the decisions, asks of you, commitments, deadlines and open questions (each linked to its email) and tells you where things stand. Each project is read back to you, with what it would have filed in the last 90 days, before it's saved. Spam, phishing and one-time codes are never filed.
